03 / DOSSIER OPERATIONAL · CHAPTER METHOD
METHOD

STRATEGY IS PROTOCOL

Three movements. In sequence. Never improvised. Diagnosis maps the terrain — Structure builds the system — Execution places the move.

01.
THE PROTOCOL
PHASE · 01 WEEKS 1–3

Diagnosis

Before any movement, the terrain is mapped. We audit the brand, study the market, and identify the structural gaps.

  • Brand audit · narrative, positioning, voice
  • Market mapping · competition, white space
  • Audience study · behaviour, friction, motive
  • Gap report · what's missing, what's leaking
DELIVERABLE Strategic Diagnostic Report
PHASE · 02 WEEKS 3–6

Structure

Diagnosis becomes architecture. We translate findings into a brand system, a growth model, and a 90-day operational plan.

  • Brand system · positioning, voice, visual code
  • Growth model · funnel, channels, signal map
  • Content architecture · pillars & cadence
  • 90-day operational roadmap
DELIVERABLE Brand Operations Playbook
PHASE · 03 ONGOING · QUARTERLY

Execution

Now the move is placed. Content, paid media, and measurement run inside the structure — not around it.

  • Content production · graphics, reels, photography
  • Paid media · audience build, conversion
  • Community & comms · channel-by-channel
  • Measurement · monthly diagnostic refresh
