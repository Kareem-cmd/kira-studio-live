Type & visual exploration / 2025

Funny Brands

Bullseye

Bullseye is an entertainment brand specialized in interactive activities such as axe throwing, archery, and balloon popping challenges. It targets youth and families who are seeking exciting, adventurous, and competitive experiences. The brand creates a dynamic and energetic atmosphere, blending fun with competition in a safe and engaging environment.

Adventurous: Encourages new, thrilling experiences. Energetic: Always lively and full of action. Competitive: Inspires friendly rivalry among friends and families. Youthful: Fresh, modern, and connected to the younger generation.

Social Media ADs

Pluto Store

Pluto Store is an online toy and accessories shop that offers a wide range of fun, creative, and trendy products. From plush toys inspired by popular movies to kids’ accessories and playful gifts, Pluto Store focuses on bringing joy and excitement to children and families. The store combines imagination, playfulness, and convenience, delivering products that make every moment memorable.

Brand Personality Playful: Fun, joyful, and lighthearted. Creative: Always offering unique and imaginative products. Family-friendly: Safe and appealing for both kids and parents. Trendy: Follows what’s popular and loved by kids.

Social Media Ads

Creative Posts

Visual Identity Colors: Bright and cheerful tones (light blue, pink, pastel shades) to reflect positivity and joy. Style: Cute, fun, and visually attractive with cartoon-like touches. Mood: Friendly, playful, and designed to capture children’s attention. Elements: Toys, animated icons, gift vibes, and fun packaging.

GIF

Teaser Post – Pluto Store This post was designed as a teaser campaign using a creative blend of real-life movie elements and animated plush toys (Stitch & Angel).The concept relied on visual merging to create a cinematic atmosphere, making the toys feel like part of the movie experience. 🎯 Results: Achieved very high organic reach due to its engaging and playful design. Strong audience interaction driven by nostalgia, cuteness, and creativity. Successfully positioned Pluto Store as a fun and trendy brand for kids’ toys and gifts.

Campaign Post – Pluto Store These two promotional posts were created as part of a cinema-themed campaign for Stitch & Angel plush toys. The creative concept plays on the idea of watching movies with your favorite plush companion, making the experience more immersive and emotional for the audience. 🖌️ Creative Approach: Used playful and catchy headlines like “Don’t go to the movie without Stitch” and “Watching Stitch without Stitch doesn’t feel right”. Integrated cinema-related visuals to strengthen the storytelling. Highlighted offers & benefits (Free Delivery + Affordable Price) in a clear and appealing design. 🎯 Results: Captured attention with its relatable and humorous concept. Drove high engagement thanks to the emotional link between the product and the movie experience. Reinforced Pluto Store’s positioning as a creative and fun toy shop.

Fun Vibes

Project Overview Fun Vibes is a playful lifestyle brand that brings creativity and joy into everyday fashion. With bold designs inspired by food culture – from Pizza Socks to Donut Socks – the brand transforms ordinary products into eye-catching, fun statements. This project was developed as part of a branding and social media campaign, focusing on colorful visuals, witty taglines, and a modern lifestyle aesthetic to connect with a young, trend-driven audience.

Advertising Campaign – Fun Vibes Campaign Overview As part of Fun Vibes’ product launch, a series of digital ads were created to promote playful and trendy items like Pizza Socks, Donut Socks, and colorful slippers. Each ad was designed with a strong visual identity, vibrant colors, and catchy headlines to maximize attention and engagement.

Results & Highlights Created a strong ad identity consistent across all products. Balanced fun storytelling with clear promotional messaging. Designed to attract both young consumers and families looking for trendy, gift-worthy items.
