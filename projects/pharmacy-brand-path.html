Brand identity / 2025

Pharmacy Brand - Path

Brand: Sabeel Pharmacy — Saudi pharmacy brand blending trust, convenience, and modern delivery solutions. One‑liner (EN): Trusted pharmacy services with fast delivery, easy payment plans, and best‑selling products curated for your needs. Tone: Trustworthy · Accessible · Modern Category: Pharmacy / Health & Beauty

Objective: Reinforce brand reliability while driving seasonal offers (Buy 1 Get 1, Installments, Delivery, Best Sellers) through high‑impact visuals. Audience: Residents in Saudi Arabia seeking accessible pharmacy services and convenience. Design DNA: Futuristic pharmacy setting; dynamic neon trails; product hero shots; confident pharmacist figures; bold Arabic headlines. Materials: Photography of actual products; 3D light streaks; pharmacy interiors; clean typography. Palette: Teal #1AB1AA, White #F8FCFB, Charcoal #1C1F23, Accent Magenta #D83A87. USP: Fast delivery, flexible payment, trusted service, curated best sellers. Voice: Professional, clear, approachable. Deliverables: Offer banners (Buy 1 Get 1, Installments, Delivery, Best Sellers), IG carousels, web hero images, campaign reels.
