Linepack Branding

Linepack is a comprehensive branding and packaging project aimed at delivering a bold visual identity. The project features a strong logo construction, carefully selected typography, and a striking corporate color palette (Red, White, and Black). The visual experience is enhanced through dynamic motion graphics that bring the brand to life.
